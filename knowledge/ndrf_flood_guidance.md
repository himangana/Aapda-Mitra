# NDRF flood safety knowledge base (hackathon corpus)

Source: National Disaster Response Force, Government of India — [Flood safety
do's and don'ts](https://ndrf.gov.in/sites/default/files/English%20SSP.pdf).
Retrieved 2026-08-13. This compact, caller-facing corpus deliberately contains
only safety guidance suitable for the prototype; it is **not** an operational
dispatch SOP.

## Moving water and evacuation

- Do not attempt to walk or drive through fast-moving flood water.
- Move to a safe place / strong house and follow official evacuation orders.
- Listen to official warnings and instructions on radio or television while
  travelling or preparing to evacuate.

## Electricity and water hazards

- Do not enter flood water: it may be contaminated or electrically charged.
- Stay away from electric poles and fallen power lines; do not use electrical
  appliances until they have been checked.
- Use torches, lanterns, or emergency lights instead of candles.

## Food, water, children and after-flood precautions

- Use boiled or chlorinated drinking water; keep food covered and do not eat
  food affected by flood water.
- Do not allow children to go near or play in flood water.
- Before entering a house after flooding, check its condition and use a stick
  to check for snakes or other dangers.

## Demo safety boundary

Guidance helps a caller reduce immediate risk; emergency dispatch remains a
human dispatcher decision. The app must never claim to contact responders.
